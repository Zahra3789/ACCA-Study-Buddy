26 Interpretation of FS .................. I1(a) I1(b) I2(a) I2(b) I3(a) I3(b)  [calculate ratios; explain differences between years/companies; purpose and limitations; Section A or B]
   Ratio definitions used: GP margin = GP/Revenue; Operating margin = PBIT/Revenue; ROCE = PBIT/(Total equity + NCL); ROE = (Profit - pref divs)/Total equity;
   Current = CA/CL; Quick = (CA - Inventories)/CL; Inventory days = Inv/COS x 365; Receivable days = TR/Revenue x 365; Payable days = TP/COS x 365;
   Working capital cycle = inv days + rec days - pay days; Asset turnover = Revenue/(Total assets - CL); Interest cover = PBIT/Interest; Gearing = NCL/(Total equity + NCL).
